Construction and rules

Construction
  • Head 72 × 60, corners 17; antenna 11 with a 3.6 ball; eye pills 9 × 13, 20 apart.
  • 48px and up: the full head with antenna. 32px and below: the favicon head. Never below 16.
  • Ink for the body, canvas for the face. Desk and monitor in secondary ink and rules; diff colours only on its screen.
Do
  • Use it where the page waits, recovers or ends.
  • Keep it ink and canvas; one expression per moment, with words beside it.
  • Hide it from screen readers when the words already say it; stop motion under reduced motion and offscreen.
Don't
  • Put it next to code being read, in threads or in the step list.
  • Colour it with the accent or a status colour; add props, outfits, gradients or shadows.
  • Let it speak the AI's findings, or loop it where nothing is waiting.
